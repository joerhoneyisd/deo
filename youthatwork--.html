Program Overview: Helping young people work, earn, and grow

Youth@Work gives LA County’s future workforce first-time, paid work experiences in high-growth and high-road sectors, building skills that meet the region’s talent needs now and in the future.

LA County youth ages 14 to 24 get personal enrichment and hands-on paid work experience through America’s Job Centers of California (AJCC) and local employer partners.

2026 Program Review and Redesign Report (PDF)

Key Benefits: What Youth@Work offers

Paid work experience

160 hours of personal enrichment training and paid work experience for youth across LA County.

Career readiness support

Paid training, mentorship, and hands-on experience in high-demand industries.

Equity-centered opportunities

Support for youth affected by economic and climate crises and by systemic barriers, including foster, unhoused, LGBTQ+, and justice-involved youth.

Pathways into high-demand industries

Training aligned with regional workforce needs in healthcare, infrastructure, technology, and creative sectors.

Leadership and professional growth

Workshops on leadership, career planning, and peer support build confidence and lasting networks.

Partnerships with employers

Employers get access to an emerging talent pipeline for up to four months of part-time work, with wage and insurance subsidies.

Eligibility Criteria: Who can take part

Youth@Work serves LA County youth and the employers who host them.

Youth

  • LA County resident
  • Ages 14 to 24 at time of enrollment
  • Legal right to work in the U.S.
  • High-need communities
  • Current or former foster youth
  • Impacted by the justice system
  • Currently or previously experiencing homelessness
  • Identify as LGBTQ+

Employers

  • Located in LA County
  • Must meet with an AJCC to enroll as a participating employer
  • Complete a host site agreement with a detailed training plan
  • Provide managerial oversight and a point of contact
  • Validate hours worked by participants

DEO is especially looking for employers in the region’s high-growth sectors that offer pathways to hire.

Frequently Asked Questions: Common questions

Who is eligible for Youth@Work?

Youth must live in LA County, be 14 to 24 at the time of enrollment, and have the legal right to work in the U.S. The program focuses on supporting youth from high-need communities, including current or former foster youth, youth impacted by the justice system, youth currently or previously experiencing homelessness, and LGBTQ+ youth.

Is the work paid?

Yes. Participants gain paid work experience with a participating employer host site: 140 hours in the traditional, year-round program.

What does the program include?

Personal enrichment training through DEO’s Transition Age Youth – World of Work class with an AJCC (life skills, career pathway planning, and job portfolio development), paid work experience, supportive services and mentorship, and a post-program connection to additional hiring, training, and education opportunities.

How do I apply?

Fill out the Youth Interest Form. AJCC staff will reach out within 48 hours.

What industries are involved?

DEO works with employers in high-growth sectors: Aerospace and Defense, Life Sciences, Construction and Infrastructure, Creative Sector, Early Care and Education, Healthcare, Hospitality and Tourism, Information Technology and Trade, and Transportation and Logistics.

How can my business become a host site?

Email hireayouth@opportunity.lacounty.gov. AJCC staff will reach out within 48 hours to help you enroll, complete a host site agreement with a training plan, and name a point of contact.

What do employers get?

Access to a new and emerging talent pipeline for up to four months of part-time work, with wage and insurance subsidies.

What happens after the program?

Youth are connected to additional hiring, training, and education opportunities, and to employment in high-growth sectors.
